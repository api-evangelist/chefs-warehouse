Late summer brings long days, high temperatures, and increased demand for 
cold drinks and fresh food. For restaurants, bars, and foodservice 
operators, keeping products at safe and refreshing temperatures isn’t just 
about guest comfort—it’s about efficiency, food safety, and profitability. 
The right refrigeration and ice solutions can make all the difference when 
the heat is on.
