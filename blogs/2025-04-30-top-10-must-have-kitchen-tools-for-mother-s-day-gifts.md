Mother’s Day is the perfect time to show appreciation for the moms who have 
stirred, sautéed, and served up love in the kitchen for years. This year, 
skip the flowers and give her something she’ll use every day—a tool that 
makes cooking easier, faster, and more enjoyable. Whether she's a seasoned 
home chef or just loves experimenting with new recipes, these 10 kitchen 
essentials are guaranteed to make her smile.
