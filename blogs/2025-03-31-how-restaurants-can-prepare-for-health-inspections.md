Health inspections are an essential part of running a restaurant. They 
ensure food safety, protect customers from health risks, and maintain a 
restaurant’s reputation. Failing an inspection can result in fines, 
closures, or loss of customer trust. Here’s how restaurants can prepare for 
a health inspection and maintain compliance with food safety regulations.
